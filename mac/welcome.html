SKAZI Bundle by Asher Swissa

This installs every SKAZI plugin:

  • SKAZI: synth
  • SUBICH: bass and sub bass synth
  • BAMBA: risers and FX
  • SHAKSUKA: random chop and glitch FX
  • PITA PUMP: sidechain LFO tool
  • TAHINI: space delay and reverb
  • ZHUG: saturator and widener
  • BULLY: wavetable synth with warps, mod matrix, chaos and FX rack
  • KICKASS: kick drum synth with a browser for your Splice kicks
  • SALAT: glitch multi-FX: tempo-synced chopper, drive, crush, filter, ring mod, flanger, delay and reverb
  • TBON 303 (listed as SKAZI 303): acid bass line synth with 303 filter, accent, slide, 14 drives and a step sequencer
  • MIDI GOD: MIDI pattern generator for 10 genres and 9 roles, plus audio to MIDI (melody, bass, drums, chords)
  • KUBE: psytrance wavetable synth with 24 KUBE-only tables, Serum .wav import, two warp slots per oscillator and 16-voice unison
  • QUAZI: 4-band fuzz with SOFT, HARD, FUZZ and TUBE per band, stereo width with mono bass, and a 3-band post EQ
  • The FilterFish: filter FX

Close your DAW before installing. Afterwards, open it and rescan plugins: they are all under SKAZI.

To remove everything later, run Uninstall SKAZI Bundle in Applications > SKAZI.
